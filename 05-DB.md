# Database Design - Kembang Books

**Project:** Kembang Books single-brand online bookstore
**Database:** MySQL
**Framework:** Laravel 13
**ORM:** Eloquent

## 1. Core Entities

```text
User
Category (genre)
Product (book)
ProductVariant (edition and stock)
ProductImage (cover/gallery)
Address
Cart
CartItem
Wishlist
WishlistItem
Order
OrderItem
Payment
PaymentWebhook
Shipment
Voucher
Promotion
Review
ChatConversation
ChatMessage
```

## 2. Relationships

```text
User
 ├── addresses
 ├── cart
 ├── wishlist
 ├── orders
 ├── reviews
 └── chat conversations

Category
 └── products
       ├── variants / editions
       ├── images / covers
       ├── reviews
       └── order items

Cart
 └── cart items -> product variants

Order
 ├── order items
 ├── payment -> payment webhooks
 └── shipment

Chat conversation
 └── chat messages
```

## 3. Product and Category Fields

`categories`

- `id`, `name`, `slug`, `description`, `image`, `is_active`, timestamps

`products`

- `id`, `category_id`, `name`, `slug`, `sku`
- `author`, `description`, `material` (paper/format metadata)
- `price`, `compare_price`, `is_active`, `is_featured`, `views_count`
- timestamps

`product_variants`

- `id`, `product_id`, `sku`, `name`
- `format` (`paperback`, `hardcover`, `gift_edition`)
- `price`, `stock`, `is_active`, timestamps

`product_images`

- `id`, `product_id`, `image`, `alt_text`, `sort_order`, `is_primary`, timestamps

## 4. Customer and Commerce Fields

`users`: name, email, phone, password, role (`admin`/`customer`), email verification, timestamps.

`addresses`: user, label, recipient name, phone, province, city, district, postal code, full address, default flag.

`carts`: user/session, status, timestamps.

`cart_items`: cart, product variant, quantity, unit price snapshot, unique cart/variant pair.

`wishlists`: unique user ownership.

`wishlist_items`: wishlist, product, unique wishlist/product pair.

`orders`: user, unique order number, address snapshot, subtotal, discount, shipping, total, voucher, status, notes, placed time.

`order_items`: order, product, variant, title snapshot, edition snapshot, SKU, price, quantity, subtotal.

`payments`: order, gateway reference, merchant reference, channel, amount, status, paid/expired times, raw response.

`shipments`: order, courier, service, tracking number, shipping cost, estimate, status, timestamps.

## 5. Status Values

Order: `pending`, `processing`, `packed`, `shipped`, `completed`, `cancelled`.

Payment: `unpaid`, `pending`, `paid`, `failed`, `expired`, `refunded`.

Shipment: `pending`, `processing`, `shipped`, `delivered`, `returned`.

Chat: `open`, `closed`.

## 6. Business Rules

1. One customer owns one active cart and one wishlist.
2. A product variant represents a purchasable book edition.
3. Stock is held at variant level; zero stock cannot be purchased.
4. Cart quantity cannot exceed current stock.
5. Order and order item data preserve snapshots so later catalog edits do not change history.
6. Payment webhooks must be validated and idempotent.
7. Review eligibility requires a completed order containing the book.
8. Reviews are hidden until approved.
9. Customer queries must be scoped to the authenticated owner.
10. Orders should be cancelled, not physically deleted, once they have transaction history.

## 7. Recommended Indexes and Constraints

- Unique: users.email, categories.slug, products.slug, products.sku, product_variants.sku.
- Unique: carts.user_id/status, wishlists.user_id, cart_items.cart_id/product_variant_id, wishlist_items.wishlist_id/product_id.
- Unique: orders.order_number, payments.order_id, payments.reference, payments.merchant_ref, shipments.order_id.
- Index: product category/status, variant product/status, orders user/status, payment status, review product/approval, chat conversation/sender.

## 8. Seed Data

Seed at least four genres and six readable demo titles such as `The Quiet Atlas`, `Letters From Tomorrow`, `The Useful Day`, `Build Better Ideas`, `The Wild Garden`, and `Stories for Slow Sundays`. Include paperback and hardcover editions, low-stock and out-of-stock variants, approved/pending reviews, active/expired vouchers, and orders in multiple states.
